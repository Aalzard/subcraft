# Mesh-map baking: CPU baseline and GPU timings

Seconds per map, each map baked alone (so each time includes UV rasterisation and projection),
default settings (AO 32 rays, thickness 16 rays, bent normal 32 rays), low on low (no high poly).
Machine: Windows 11, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 (DX12), release build. Tool:
`cargo run --release -p subcraft-bake --example bake_bench -- <crate|torus100k|torus500k> <size> [map]`
(the generated meshes are tori; the crate is the built-in sample crate).

## CPU baseline (session 109, before GPU baking)

| Mesh | Size | Normal | World Space Normal | Position | ID | Ambient Occlusion | Curvature | Thickness | Height | Bent Normal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| crate (1,008 tris) | 2048 | 0.79 | 0.79 | 3.75 | 0.74 | 27.37 | 5.42 | 28.29 | 3.23 | 35.11 |
| crate (1,008 tris) | 4096 | 3.13 | 13.60 | 7.33 | 15.21 | 106.48 | 13.52 | 64.69 | 11.79 | 73.63 |
| torus100k (100,000 tris) | 2048 | 0.88 | 0.93 | 1.94 | 0.88 | 33.63 | 30.47 | 35.54 | 1.94 | 29.02 |
| torus100k (100,000 tris) | 4096 | 4.22 | 7.99 | 7.43 | 8.24 | 136.38 | 131.74 | 177.08 | 7.46 | 125.03 |
| torus500k (499,520 tris) | 2048 | 1.06 | 1.03 | 2.14 | 2.33 | 35.76 | 106.78 | 39.42 | 2.03 | 28.82 |
| torus500k (499,520 tris) | 4096 | 6.06 | 7.81 | 8.75 | 8.22 | 139.40 | 440.32 | 236.05 | 6.56 | 129.79 |

Notes: the first rows (crate) ran while a background build was using the machine at low priority,
so their cheap maps (Position, ID, Height, World Space Normal) are noisy by a few seconds; the
ray maps are not affected much. The CPU time of Curvature grows with the triangle count
(100k: 131 s, 500k: 440 s at 4096), AO and thickness with the covered texel count.

## With GPU baking (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060, DX12)

`cargo run --release -p subcraft-ui --example gpu_bake_bench -- <mesh> <size> [map] [cpu]`. Seconds
for the whole single-map bake, including the work that stays on the CPU (UV rasterisation,
projection, gutter padding: about 1 s at 2048 and 5 to 8 s at 4096 for any map). Speed-up is CPU
time divided by GPU time of the same bake.

| Mesh | Size | AO CPU | AO GPU | Curvature CPU | Curvature GPU | Thickness CPU | Thickness GPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| crate (1,008 tris) | 2048 | 27.4 | 3.1 (8.8x) | 5.4 | 1.6 (3.4x) | 28.3 | 1.8 (15.7x) |
| crate | 4096 | 106.5 | 8.9 (12x) | 13.5 | 6.9 (2.0x) | 64.7 | 7.3 (8.9x) |
| torus100k | 2048 | 33.6 | 3.0 (11x) | 30.5 | 2.3 (13x) | 35.5 | 2.5 (14x) |
| torus100k | 4096 | 136.4 | 9.4 (14.5x) | 131.7 | 8.5 (15x) | 177.1 | 9.4 (19x) |
| torus500k | 2048 | 35.8 | 3.7 (9.7x) | 106.8 | 3.1 (34x) | 39.4 | 2.8 (14x) |
| torus500k | 4096 | 139.4 | 11.1 (12.6x) | 440.3 | 11.1 (40x) | 236.1 | 10.7 (22x) |

Normal, World Space Normal, Position, ID and Height stay on the CPU (1 to 9 s each, unchanged).
Bent Normal also stays on the CPU (15 to 140 s, same as before): it uses the same rays as AO and is
the obvious next map to move. Of the GPU time in a bake of 500k triangles at 4096, the dispatches
themselves are about 2 s per map; the rest is the CPU preparation.

Timings were taken while other sessions were building on the same machine, so expect a few seconds
of noise; the ratios are what matters.

## Equivalence (RTX 4060)

`cargo test -p subcraft-ui --test gpu_bake` (256x256 bakes of the sample crate, a torus, a UV
sphere and a thin slab; curvature also with a high poly): the covered texels are identical (they
come from the same CPU rasterisation), no value is NaN, two GPU bakes are bit-identical. Mean
absolute error against the CPU: AO 0.002 (p99 0.031) on the crate, 0 on the primitives;
thickness 0.002 (p99 0.026) on the crate, 0.001 on the slab; curvature 0.0000 everywhere it was
compared. Stated tolerance in the test: mean 0.004, p99 0.08 (curvature 0.01 / 0.1).
One exception, recorded in the test: curvature at the south pole of `uv_sphere` (a fan of
sliver triangles about 1e-7 wide) differs, because the value there is numerical noise on the CPU
too; that mesh is left out of the curvature comparison.
