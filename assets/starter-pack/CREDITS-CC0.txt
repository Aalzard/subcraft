SubCraft starter pack

110 normal maps and 84 brush alpha maps (194 images) supplied by Aalzard for SubCraft:
the original 30 + 30, 50 + 50 procedurally generated in a second pack (texture_pack_2), and 30 + 4 made with an image generator (2026-11; normals 1024 px, alphas 512 px).
