SubCraft bundled environments

The 10 HDR environments bundled with SubCraft (Alpine Lake Blue Hour, Atelier Windows,
Paint Booth, Ceramic Gallery, Neon Arcade, Glasshouse Morning, Orbital Atrium, Redwood
Creek, Salt Flats Twilight, Library Hall) are AI-generated panoramas whose HDR radiance was
synthesized from the generated images with artist-chosen lighting values. They are not
photographs and not measured HDR captures. No downloaded or stock imagery was used.
The bundled files are 1024 x 512 downscales of the 2048 x 1024 originals, which are
available as the extra-environments download on the GitHub release page.
